Job Hunt HQ company dataset

Static JSON files showing which US and Canadian employers have filed to sponsor foreign workers. They are built from public US Department of Labor files (LCA and PERM) and Canada's positive LMIA employers list, together with hand-written company profiles, and live under data/v1/.

These figures show past filings, not whether a company will sponsor you.
