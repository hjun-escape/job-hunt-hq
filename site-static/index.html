Job Hunt HQ company dataset

Static JSON files on which US and Canadian employers have sponsored foreign workers, built from public US Department of Labor files (LCA and PERM) and Canada's positive LMIA employers list, plus hand-written company profiles. The files live under data/v1/.

These figures show past filings, not whether a company will sponsor you.
